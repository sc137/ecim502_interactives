Cloud storage saves files on internet-connected computer systems managed by a company, school, or organization. Sign in to your account to reach your files from more than one device.

Store

Drive can hold PDFs, photos, audio, video, and Microsoft Office files as well as Google files.

Create

Google Docs is for writing. Google Sheets is for tables and lists. Both can be created in Drive.

Share

A share link leads to the online file. The access settings decide who can open it and what they can do.

A simple class routine

  1. Use your Saddleback College Google account for class work if possible. Check the account picture or initials before creating a file.
  2. Use a clear name, such as Class Packing Checklist, and a class folder you can find again.
  3. Keep copies of important files in another safe location. Syncing changes across devices is useful, but changes or deletions may sync too.
  4. Protect your account with a strong, unique password and two-step verification. Keep recovery options current.

School accounts can have different storage limits and sharing rules. Access may change after you leave college, so plan ahead for files you need to keep.

Two separate questions: who, and what?

Start with the narrowest audience and the lowest permission that meets your purpose. For this class assignment, use a low-risk practice file with Anyone with the link and Viewer. Commenter is also allowed if you want feedback.

1. Who can open it?

Restricted: people who already have access, such as people you add directly. Copying a restricted link does not grant a new person access.

School / organization: an account-dependent option for people in that organization.

Anyone with the link: anyone who receives the link can open it, including someone it is forwarded to.

2. What can they do?

Viewer: read the file without editing it.

Commenter: leave comments or suggestions without directly editing the original text.

Editor: change the file; sharing abilities depend on settings. Reserve this for trusted collaborators.

Viewer is not a privacy shield. Readers can still pass along a public link or capture what they see. A file owner’s name and email may be visible. Share only content suitable for that audience.

Check the folder, too

Sharing a folder can give people access to its contents. A file may inherit access from its folder. Check existing people and folder access as well as General access. For this assignment, share the individual practice file.

After the assignment is reviewed, change General access back to Restricted if public access is no longer needed. Review directly added people and folder permissions separately. Removing access cannot retrieve copies someone already made.

Open Google Drive (new tab)

Two ways to work: Follow along now, or review the steps for later. Your certificate records completion of this learning tool; it does not confirm a real file was shared or submitted.
  1. Open Google Drive and check your account. Use your Saddleback College account if possible.
  2. Select New → Google Docs or Google Sheets. Give the file a clear name and add a short title plus a few lines of non-private sample content.
  3. Review the content. Remove passwords, account numbers, addresses, phone numbers, medical or financial details, family information, and student records.
  4. Select Share. Under General access, choose Anyone with the link. Choose Viewer; Commenter is allowed for feedback. Do not choose Editor for the public class link.
  5. Select Copy link, then Done. If possible, open the link in a signed-out or private browser window to check that it opens for a reader. This test does not replace reviewing the sharing settings.
  6. In Canvas, open Create a Shared Doc. Paste the share link in the text-entry box, check the sharing and privacy again, and select Submit Assignment.
Missing “Anyone with the link” or seeing “Request access”? Check the signed-in account and the file’s Share settings. Your school may block external sharing. Ask Professor Sable for an approved alternative if the required option is unavailable. Do not move private school files to a personal account to bypass a restriction.
