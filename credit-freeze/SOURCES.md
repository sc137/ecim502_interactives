# Credit Freeze content review

Reviewed November 16, 2026. Primary course content: the supplied **How-to-Freeze-Your-Credit.md** and **Identity-Theft-and-Fraud-Targeting-Seniors.md**. The interactive adapts the handouts’ freeze workflow, record-keeping advice, scam red flags, independent verification, and recovery steps. It intentionally concentrates on the three major bureaus rather than the optional specialty-reporting freezes.

Official verification:

- [FTC: Credit freezes and fraud alerts](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/credit-freezes-and-fraud-alerts): free freezes; no credit-score effect; separate requests; alert eligibility, durations, and one-bureau notification; freezes and alerts can coexist.
- [USAGov: Place or lift a freeze](https://www.usa.gov/credit-freeze): online, phone, and mail methods; placement within one business day online/phone or three by mail; lifting within one hour online/phone or three business days by mail.
- [Equifax freeze instructions](https://www.equifax.com/personal/credit-report-services/credit-freeze/): myEquifax management, free placement/lifting/removal, representative processes, and **888-298-0045**. This replaces the handout’s 800-685-1111 for freeze management, using Equifax’s own current instructions.
- [Experian freeze instructions](https://www.experian.com/help/credit-freeze/): free account, scheduled thaw, freeze status, no PIN needed for online management, phone **888-397-3742**, mail instructions, and distinction from paid CreditLock. The old handout URL redirects here.
- [TransUnion freeze instructions](https://www.transunion.com/credit-freeze): free Service Center account and temporary lifts. The public information page replaces the handout’s direct enrollment URL.
- [TransUnion phone/mail instructions](https://www.transunion.com/credit-freeze/mail-or-phone): automated freeze management at **888-909-8872**; document and mailing instructions.
- [FTC: Identity theft](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-know-about-identity-theft): credit versus identity monitoring, coverage gaps, recovery assistance, insurance limits, and free recovery help.
- [FTC: Free credit reports](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/free-credit-reports): official report access and checking for errors or unfamiliar accounts.

No particular monitoring vendor is endorsed. Costs depend on the plan; the $15/$25 comparison is explicitly fictional, not a current product quotation. Avoid screenshots or exact menu paths that would imply bureau interfaces cannot change. Reverify contacts, request timing, and product terms during substantive updates.

## Privacy and release checks

No personal-information fields, account authentication, analytics, network requests from lesson JavaScript, cookies, or browser storage are used by this lesson. All simulated freezes, answers, and exploration state are kept in page memory and disappear on reload. Certificate uses a generic learner label. External official links leave the lesson and have their own privacy practices. Existing interactives retain their prior behavior; only their header stylesheet link changes.

This is a buildless HTML/CSS/JavaScript tool. Production follows the repository’s existing GitHub Pages URLs. Include the sibling `shared/` folder when copying the tool elsewhere.
