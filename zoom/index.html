Zoom Workplace Basics: What Every Student Should Know

💡 Big Idea: Zoom is More Than a Meeting Link

Zoom Workplace is Zoom's complete collaboration platform. It includes familiar video meeting tools, plus connected tools for team chat, document sharing, whiteboards, short videos, notes, tasks, and scheduling.

For class, the most essential skills are joining class meetings, managing your microphone and camera, using chat, viewing or sharing screens, and knowing when desktop or mobile is best.

Core Zoom Workplace Tools

Overview of primary Zoom Workplace features
Tool Name What it is for What students should recognize
📹 Meetings Live video, audio, screen sharing, live captions, reactions, and class sessions. This is the part most people mean when they say "Join Zoom."
💬 Team Chat Messages, files, group conversations, and follow-up between meetings. Zoom has chat inside meetings, plus ongoing chat outside meetings in Zoom Workplace.
🎨 Whiteboard A shared visual canvas for brainstorming, sticky notes, diagrams, and group planning. Can be used live during a meeting or opened later for group work.
🎬 Clips Short video or screen recordings shared without holding another live meeting. Useful when someone wants to explain a process or show a screen update quickly.
📑 Docs, Notes & Tasks Written work, meeting notes, action items, and follow-up planning. Helps turn a live discussion into action items you can review later.
📅 Calendar & Scheduler Meeting planning, booking pages, reminders, and calendar connections. Availability depends on your specific account and connected services.

Essential Classroom Meeting Controls

🎤 Mute / Unmute

Turns your microphone off or on. Keep yourself muted when not speaking to reduce background noise for your classmates.

📹 Start / Stop Video

Turns your camera off or on. Choose whether your background and face are visible to the class.

👥 Participants

Opens a panel showing everyone who is currently in the Zoom meeting room.

💬 Chat

Opens the meeting chat window so you can type questions, read links, or respond to the instructor.

🖥️ Share Screen

Lets you show a document, browser tab, or your whole screen to the class if the host allows it.

✋ Reactions

Lets you raise your hand virtually or give quick visual feedback (thumbs up, applause) without interrupting.

CC Captions

Displays spoken words as text at the bottom of your screen when enabled by the host.

⚙️ More Options

Opens extra controls such as audio settings, full screen mode, or background filters.

Classroom Privacy & Etiquette Basics
  • Mute when not speaking: Protects class audio quality from pets, fans, or household noise.
  • Share only what you intend: When sharing your screen, select a specific window or tab instead of your entire desktop.
  • Check your background: Use a tidy space or a virtual background if you prefer to keep your personal room private.
  • Keep Zoom updated: Regular updates ensure you have security patches and the latest accessible tools.

What's New with Zoom: The AI Workplace Evolution

The Big Shift in Zoom

Zoom is expanding from "a place to hold live video calls" into an AI-supported workspace. The newest features focus on capturing what happened in class, summarizing discussions, finding information across docs, and creating follow-up notes, documents, slides, and sheets.

Interactive Timeline: How Zoom Has Evolved

2020: Basic Video Meetings
Current Zoom AI Names: Built-in AI Features and ZoomMate

Important Naming Note: Features formerly grouped under the AI Companion name—such as meeting summaries, transcription, and chat compose—are now built into Zoom Workplace and usually named by what they do.

ZoomMate is different: It is Zoom's newer agentic product for broader search, content creation, and workflow automation across Zoom and connected services.

Availability depends on your Zoom plan, administrator settings, platform, and—in some cases—AI credits. Review permissions before enabling any AI feature.

Newest Zoom AI Features to Know

Built-in Zoom AI & ZoomMate

Built-in Zoom AI features handle specific tasks such as summaries and transcription. ZoomMate adds broader search, content creation, and workflow actions.

📝 My Notes & Mobile Notes

Lets an individual take personal notes and optionally enrich them with a transcript for supported Zoom, in-person, and third-party meetings. Platform and account requirements apply.

⚡ Meeting Workflows

Turns discussion notes into follow-up tasks, emails, or action items automatically, moving from "summarizing" to "doing next steps."

🔎 Agentic Search

Searches across your Zoom meetings, chats, documents, and connected cloud drives (Google Drive, OneDrive) to answer questions in natural language.

Avatar & Clips Transparency Caution

Zoom now offers AI Avatars and AI-generated video backgrounds for Clips. While polished, AI avatars blur the line between real human recordings and generated media.

Emeritus Guidance: Always maintain transparency. For educational, health, legal, or personal financial conversations, ensure participants know when AI-generated audio or video is being used.

The 6 Privacy Questions to Ask Before Turning AI On

1. What is it capturing?

Is it recording audio, video, chat, screen shares, shared files, or meeting transcripts?

2. Who can see the output?

Will the AI summary be seen only by you, the host, all class members, or connected apps?

3. Where is it stored?

Is data saved in Zoom Hub, Zoom Canvas, local cloud recording, or a shared document?

4. Can it take action?

Is the AI only summarizing, or can it automatically send emails, tasks, or calendar invites?

5. Is the summary accurate?

AI summaries can mistake names or miss context. Always review notes before taking action.

6. Is it appropriate?

For sensitive conversations, follow the host organization's policy and any notice or consent requirements that apply before recording or transcribing.
