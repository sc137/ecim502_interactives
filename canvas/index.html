Canvas Basics: One Place for Your Course Work

Big idea: global tools and course tools are different

Global Navigation stays on the left side of Canvas and connects you to information across all your courses. Course Navigation appears after you open a course and connects you to that course’s Modules, Assignments, Discussions, Grades, and other tools selected by the instructor.

Dashboard

Your landing page. Depending on your Canvas setup, it may show course cards, a list, recent activity, or a customizable view.

Courses

Opens favorite courses and the All Courses list. A course may not appear until it is published or your enrollment becomes active.

Calendar

Combines dated items from courses you can access. Always read the assignment page because its directions are more complete than a calendar entry.

Inbox

Canvas Conversations for course messages. It is not the same as a personal email inbox.

Account

Opens settings, notifications, personal files, profile options when enabled, and logout.

Help

Shows the support choices configured by your institution. The available choices can differ from another school’s Canvas site.

A reliable weekly routine

  1. Open the course and begin with the current Module or course Home page.
  2. Read the complete assignment instructions, due date, allowed submission type, and available attempts.
  3. Use the Dashboard To Do list and Calendar as reminders, not as replacements for the course instructions.
  4. Check Inbox, Announcements, Grades, submission comments, and recent feedback.
  5. After submitting, verify that Canvas displays the submission details.
Your screen may look different

Institutions and instructors can enable, disable, rename, or reorder some tools. The Canvas Student mobile app also has a different layout. Ask your instructor or use your institution’s Help menu when a required option is missing.

Choose Canvas Notification Preferences

In Canvas, open Account → Notifications. Email notification choices are Notify immediately, Daily summary, Weekly summary, and Notifications off. A change takes effect immediately.

Choose a sustainable pattern

There is no single correct schedule for every learner. Time-sensitive course announcements, instructor submission comments, and Inbox conversations are often useful sooner. High-volume discussion activity may be easier to manage as a summary. This practice lets you choose.

Before applying your choices in Canvas

Mobile push notifications work differently

Push notifications support immediate delivery or off, not daily or weekly summaries. Settings changed inside the Canvas Student app can override browser notification settings. Available contact-method columns depend on your account and institution.

Update Your Canvas Profile Photo

A profile image can help classmates and the instructor recognize your posts and messages. It does not need to be formal, and it does not need to show your face.

Choose an image you are comfortable sharing

Current browser steps

  1. Log in to your institution’s Canvas website.
  2. In Global Navigation, select Account, then Settings.
  3. Select the profile-picture icon. If it is missing, your institution has not enabled profile-picture changes.
  4. Choose Upload a Picture, Take a Picture, or From Gravatar, if those choices are available.
  5. For an upload, choose the image, adjust the crop so the important part fits the square, and select Save.
  6. Confirm that the new image appears. Canvas stores uploaded profile images in your personal user files.
Why the assignment may say Account → Profile

If your institution enables the broader Profiles feature, Account may also include Profile and an Edit Profile button. Canvas treats that feature and permission to change a profile picture separately. The dedicated current profile-picture guide uses Account → Settings.

Submit the profile-photo assignment

  1. Open your Canvas profile or settings page after the updated image is visible.
  2. Take a screenshot that shows the updated image but excludes unrelated personal information.
  3. Open the course assignment and read its submission instructions and allowed file types.
  4. Select Start Assignment, add or upload the screenshot, then select Submit Assignment.
  5. Verify the submission details appear. If your course uses Assignment Enhancements, button names or placement may differ.
