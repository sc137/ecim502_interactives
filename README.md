# Emeritus Interactives

These interactive tools help Emeritus students explore technology topics independently or in class.

**[Canvas LMS](https://sc137.github.io/ecim502_interactives/canvas/)**

Practice finding your way around Canvas, choosing notifications, updating a profile photo, and preparing for course work.

**[Zoom](https://sc137.github.io/ecim502_interactives/zoom)**

Explore meeting controls, practice with a simulated toolbar, and review AI features and privacy choices.

**[Firefox](https://sc137.github.io/ecim502_interactives/firefox/)**

Learn how browsers work, explore their history, and follow Firefox installation instructions for your device.

**[A Little History of the World Wide Web Slideshow](https://sc137.github.io/ecim502_interactives/web-history-slideshow/)**

Follow the Web’s beginnings, from connected information at CERN to its growth into an everyday tool.

**[Using AI with a Security Mindset](https://sc137.github.io/ecim502_interactives/using-ai-security/index.html)**

Practice removing personal details from prompts, reviewing privacy controls, and checking AI answers and suspicious instructions.

**[AI Chat Prompt Examples](https://sc137.github.io/ecim502_interactives/prompt-examples/)**

Adapt practical prompt examples with clear goals, useful context, limits, and ways to check the results.

**[Backup Your Data](https://sc137.github.io/ecim502_interactives/backup-your-data/)**

Explore the 3-2-1 backup rule, test recovery scenarios, and review backup options for Windows and Mac.

**[Software Update Checklist](https://sc137.github.io/ecim502_interactives/software-update-checklist/)**

Learn why updates matter and review update steps for computers, phones, tablets, routers, and smart home devices.

**[Google Drive Sharing Interactive Guide](https://sc137.github.io/ecim502_interactives/google-drive-sharing/)**

Practice sharing permissions and privacy choices, then follow the steps to create a shared file and submit its link in Canvas.
